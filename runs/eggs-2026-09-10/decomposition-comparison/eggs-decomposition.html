Stop here before contextualizing

The generated interview starts by asking for personal risk factors. That is premature if the goal is a general, shareable evidence map. Establish the purpose first; then ask only context that changes scope or evidence matching.

The next stage will need your priorities, intended dose, realistic replacement foods, and any relevant population constraints. None have been assumed.
