# Gold decomposition examples (few-shot seed)

Human-shaped, grammar-grounded decompositions → few-shot for the model. Every dimension traces
to a **role in the sentence** (subject · verb · object · adjective · modal) AND applies the
recurring lenses below. Status: 🔨 draft · ✅ locked (shaped by operator)

## The recurring lenses (check each; not all apply)
frame/values · what-exactly · **vs-what (counterfactual)** · who/jurisdiction · where/context-of-use ·
**when/time-horizon** · how-much/number · feasibility (can-it-be-done) · cost-all-in-vs-means ·
downside/risk · world-model/future · personal-fit ·
**options-are-bundles** (each branch is a different real object/scenario — decompose EACH; don't assume they differ on one axis) ·
**constraint-cascade** (one variable, e.g. budget, forces the rest: home → neighborhood → commute) ·
**legal/regulatory regime** (rights, protections, rent-control, lock-in, taxation, council rules)

## 🔨 "Should I learn to play the guitar as an adult?" — decision · skill
| role · word | dimension | resolutions |
|---|---|---|
| frame · *should* | Worth it by what measure? | fun · reachable skill · health · social · income · opportunity cost |
| verb · *learn* | Can an adult realistically learn — how hard? | tractable · needs discipline · steep past an age · effectively no |
| verb phrase · *learn to play* | To what level, at what effort? | strum → play fluently → perform → master; hrs/wk, months–years |
| object · *the guitar* | Which instrument / which guitar? | acoustic · electric · classical · or ukulele/bass/piano |
| subject+adj · *I … as an adult* | How old, from where? | 20s · 30s–40s · 50s+ · prior experience · free time |

## ✅ "Is it worth buying an electric car?" — decision · purchase
| role · word | dimension | resolutions / factors |
|---|---|---|
| frame · *worth* + purpose | What's it actually FOR? | commute · needed for work · family · luxury/status · leisure |
| verb · *buying* | Can you afford it, all-in? | sticker + loan rates + govt EV subsidies + running cost — *vs your income/disposable income* |
| context of use | Where & how will you drive it? | dense city · highways · rough village/India roads (→ suspension) · long distance · km/week |
| adjective · *electric* → comparator | Versus what? (counterfactual) | keep current vehicle · petrol/hybrid · rent when needed · public transport · don't buy |
| object · *car* | Which electric car? | new/used · size · price tier · model & range |

## ✅ "Should I switch careers into software engineering?" — decision · career
| role · word | dimension | resolutions / factors |
|---|---|---|
| frame · *should* + goals | What do you actually want? | income · remote / work-in-USA / location freedom · time for family/partner/parents · flexibility · enjoyment |
| destination · *into software engineering* | Which kind of work, in what setting? | frontend/backend/data-ML/devops · employee vs consultant vs freelance · startup vs big-co · what you'd enjoy |
| subject · *I* (career capital) | What's your current career capital? | experience & transferable skills · what you're good at/enjoy · education · gender · network/referrals · how hiring actually works |
| verb · *switch* | Can you realistically switch — and land a job? | time/cost to job-ready · income gap · will you get hired (network) · what if it fails / you're fired |
| implied · future | Outlook under which world model? | demand next 5 yrs · your bet on AI disrupting coding · how the field evolves |
| implied · fit | Does the day-to-day suit you? | OK at a computer for hours? · want physical work? · sedentary chronic-health concern |

## ✅ "Should we build more nuclear power plants?" — policy
| role · word | dimension | resolutions / factors |
|---|---|---|
| frame · *should* (normative) | Good by which criterion / whose values? | climate · cost · energy security · safety & waste · public welfare |
| subject · *we* | Which "we" — whose decision & jurisdiction? | EU vs country vs province · what authority they hold · do we have nuclear experience/track record |
| verb+comparator · *build more* | "More" than what — baseline & increment? | how many exist now · how many more · how past builds went |
| verb · *build* | Build meaning what, over how long, funded how? | large fission vs SMR vs Gen-IV vs extend-existing · build time (20 yrs vs faster — the trade-off) · financing (loan?) · fuel sourcing · waste disposal |
| context · *where* | Build WHERE — and what do locals think? | siting/geography · local acceptance vs opposition |
| implied · counterfactual | Versus what? | vs coal/gas · vs solar/wind · alongside renewables · vs nothing |

## ✅ "Is it better to rent or buy a home?" — decision · financial  (v2 — corrected by operator)

**KEY REFRAME:** renting and buying do NOT get you the same home. Budget + market put the
rent-you and the buy-you in different places → this is **package-vs-package**, and each package
**cascades** (budget → which home → which neighborhood → commute to office & to kids).

| role · word | dimension | lens | resolutions / factors |
|---|---|---|---|
| budget (the pivot) | **What home/market does each option even get you?** | options-are-bundles + cascade | monthly rent you can afford vs down-payment+mortgage you can afford → different price points → rent-home in area A vs buy-home in area B (not the same home!) |
| context · location | **Each option's location → commute & neighborhood** | where + cascade | rent-home's commute to office + kids' school vs buy-home's · neighborhood quality / safety / amenities of each |
| object · legal regime | **Rights, protections & rules of each** | legal-regime | rent → landlord rent-hikes? lock-in period? notice/eviction? what you may modify · buy → ownership & squatting rights · property tax · council rules on use/modification |
| verb · *buy* (money) | **Money over time, all-in** | cost | the two homes' total cost incl. **rent-and-invest-the-difference** · property tax vs none · transaction & maintenance · break-even horizon |
| frame · *better* + time | **What you value & how long you'll stay** | values + time | flexibility/mobility vs stability/control · staying <3 yrs vs 10+ · life stage |
