## A · Observational CVD & mortality (cohorts, pooled, meta)

### zhong-2019 `✓` — the main dissent
- Zhong et al. 2019, *JAMA* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30874756/ · pooled 6 US cohorts · n=29,615 · 17.5 y median
- +½ egg/day → CVD **HR 1.06** (1.03–1.10), mortality 1.08 (1.04–1.11); +300 mg chol/day → CVD **1.17** (1.09–1.26). **Egg signal became NON-SIGNIFICANT after adjusting for dietary cholesterol** (self-verified quote).
- funding NIH · COI — · **H ✓** · bears_on: F1-CVD, crux(cholesterol-not-egg)

### drouin-chartier-2020-bmj — the main null + substitution
- Drouin-Chartier et al. 2020, *BMJ* 368:m513 — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7190072/ · 3 US cohorts (n≈215k) + meta-analysis (**1.72M**)
- ≥1 egg/day vs <1/mo → CVD **HR 0.93** (0.82–1.05, NULL); meta RR 0.98 (0.93–1.03). **Substitution:** egg→red/processed meat or full-fat milk = worse; egg→fish/poultry/legumes/nuts/grains = neutral. Regional: US 1.01, Europe 1.05, **Asia 0.92** (inverse).
- funding NIH · COI 🥚-adjacent (one author honoraria Dairy Farmers of Canada) · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, substitution

### hu-1999 — null general, diabetic harm
- Hu et al. 1999, *JAMA* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10217054/ · HPFS+NHS · n=117,933
- ≥1/day vs <1/wk → men RR 1.08 (ns), women 0.82 (ns). **Diabetic subgroup: CHD RR 2.02** (1.05–3.87) men.
- funding NIH · COI — · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, diabetes-subgroup

### rong-2013 — dose-response meta, diabetic harm
- Rong et al. 2013, *BMJ* 346:e8539 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23295181/ · meta, 8 articles/17 reports
- +1 egg/day → CHD RR 0.99 (0.85–1.15), stroke 0.91 (0.81–1.02), both NULL. **Diabetic subgroup CHD RR 1.54** (1.14–2.09).
- funding NIH training grant · COI — (clean) · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, diabetes-subgroup

### pure-2020 — global null
- Dehghan et al. 2020 (PURE), *AJCN* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31965140/ · ~177k, 50 countries
- ≥7/wk vs <1/wk → CVD HR 0.92 (0.83–1.01), mortality 1.04 (0.94–1.15). All NULL.
- funding CIHR + **heavy pharma platform funding** · COI 💊 · **H** (HRs); M (funding wording) · bears_on: F1-CVD

### china-kadoorie-2018 — inverse (context-flipped)
- Qin et al. 2018, *Heart* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6241631/ · n=461,213 Chinese adults · 8.9 y
- Daily vs never/rare → CVD **HR 0.89** (0.87–0.92), all protective. Egg intake in China tracks *higher* SES/health — mirror-image healthy-user confounding.
- funding Wellcome/NSFC · COI — · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, confounding(sign-flip)

### epic-2019 — inverse but reverse-causation
- Key et al. 2019, *Circulation* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6629175/ · EPIC, 9 countries, n=409,885
- +20 g/day → IHD HR 0.93 (0.88–0.99), **but attenuates to null when first 4 y excluded — authors attribute to reverse causation.**
- funding MRC/CRUK/BHF · COI — · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, confounding

### zhuang-2021 — harm + substitution + mediation
- Zhuang et al. 2021, *PLOS Med* — https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1003508 · NIH-AARP, n=521,120 · 16 y
- +½ whole egg/day → all-cause **HR 1.07** (1.06–1.08); **63.2% mediated by cholesterol intake.** Substitution ½ egg→nuts/legumes **−13%** mortality; →fish/poultry/dairy −7 to −9%; →egg whites −6%.
- funding China NKRDP · COI — · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, substitution

### zhao-2022 — harm, regional heterogeneity
- Zhao et al. 2022, *Circulation* 145:1506 — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9134263/ · meta, 49 estimates, 3.6M
- +50 g egg/day → CVD RR 1.04 (1.00–1.08). **US 1.08** (1.02–1.14); **Asia 0.96** (0.87–1.06, null). Own cohort is male European smokers. Contested by Song et al. 2022 Letter (*Circulation* 146:e299).
- funding intramural NCI · COI — · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, confounding

## B · Recent systematic/umbrella reviews (2021–2025)

### godos-2021 — null CVD, HF signal
- Godos et al. 2021, *Eur J Nutr* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8137614/ · 39 studies, ~2M
- Up to 1 egg/day → CVD SRR 0.94 (0.89–0.99, mildly protective); stroke null. **Heart failure at 1/day SRR 1.15** (1.02–1.30).
- funding not extracted (verify) · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, hidden-signal(HF)

### krittanawong-2021 — null CVD
- Krittanawong et al. 2021, *Am J Med* — https://www.amjmed.com/article/S0002-9343(20)30549-0/abstract · 23 studies, 1.4M
- >1/day → CVD "not associated"; CAD HR 0.89 (0.86–0.93) but authors say "may be healthy-user bias."
- funding not extracted · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD

### mousavi-2022 — null CVD-mortality, cancer signal
- Mousavi et al. 2022, *Adv Nutr* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9526855/ · 33 cohorts, 2.2M
- CVD mortality highest-v-lowest RR 1.04 (0.87–1.23, NULL). **Cancer mortality RR 1.20** (1.04–1.39). Recommends ≤1 egg/day.
- funding not extracted · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, hidden-signal(cancer)

### umbrella-2025 — "no association," critically-low quality
- 2025 umbrella review, *Nutr Metab Cardiovasc Dis* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39934049/ · 14 meta-analyses
- "No evidence of association" for CVD/all-cause mortality. HF RR 1.15 (1.02–1.30); cancer mortality 1.13 (1.06–1.20). LDL **WMD +7.39 mg/dL** (5.82–8.95). **Underlying reviews rated "critically low" (AMSTAR-2).**
- funding not extracted · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, evidence-quality, mechanism/LDL

### diabegg — the RCT counterpoint `🥚`
- Fuller et al. 2015/2018, *AJCN* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25833969/ · RCT, n=140 prediabetes/T2D · 3–12 mo
- High-egg (~12/wk) vs low: HDL diff +0.02 mmol/L (P=0.38); "no between-group differences" in TC/LDL/TG. Concludes eggs "can be included safely." **A lipid-endpoint RCT does NOT reproduce the cohort harm → points to confounding, not lipid mechanism.**
- funding **Australian Egg Corporation** · COI 🥚 · **H** (numbers), M (funding) · bears_on: diabetes-subgroup, COI

## D · Lipid mechanism, dose-response & hyper-responders

### weggemans-2001 — independent dose-response anchor
- Weggemans et al. 2001, *AJCN* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11333841/ · meta of 17 feeding trials, n=556
- +100 mg chol/day → total:HDL ratio +0.020 (0.010–0.030); TC +2.2 mg/dL. Independent (academic).
- funding academic · COI — · **H** · bears_on: mechanism/LDL

### vincent-2019 — LDL dose-response `🥚`
- Vincent et al. 2019, *AJCN* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30596814/ · meta-regression of RCTs
- +100 mg chol/day → **LDL +1.9 mg/dL** (1.6–2.3), HDL +0.5, TC +2.4. Note: ENC-funded analysis that nonetheless confirms the LDL rise, but frames it as "modest."
- funding **Egg Nutrition Center** · COI 🥚 · **H** · bears_on: mechanism/LDL, COI

### li-kang-2020 — independent RCT meta, clear LDL rise
- Li et al. 2020, *Nutrients* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7400894/ · meta of 17 RCTs
- More egg → **LDL +8.14 mg/dL** (4.46–11.82); LDL:HDL +0.14 (0.05–0.22). Independent (hospital-funded) — confirms physiology.
- funding Wan Fang Hospital · COI — · **H** · bears_on: mechanism/LDL

### blesso-2013 — HDL rise `🥚`
- Blesso/Fernandez et al. 2013, *Metabolism* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3869568/ · RCT n=37, met-syn, carb-restricted
- 3 eggs/day (534 mg chol) → HDL +19.1% vs +9.9%; no change in LDL/TC. (Carb restriction is a co-intervention.)
- funding **Egg Nutrition Center** (Fernandez) · COI 🥚 · **H** · bears_on: mechanism/LDL, COI

### katz-njike-2015 — endothelial function null `🥚`
- Katz et al. 2015, *Am Heart J* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25497262/ · crossover RCT, n=32, CAD patients
- 2 eggs/day vs high-carb → FMD 7.2 vs 7.5% (P=0.33), TC ns. No adverse effect over 6 wk (surrogate).
- funding **Egg Nutrition Center** · COI 🥚 · **H** · bears_on: mechanism/LDL, COI

### fernandez-hyperresponders — the ~30% `🥚`
- Fernandez 2006 review + 2019 crossover — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16340654/
- ~30% "hyper-responders" (>2.2 mg/dL LDL per 100 mg chol); dietary cholesterol raises both LDL & HDL, ratio often preserved; particle shift toward larger (less atherogenic) LDL.
- funding ENC-adjacent (author COI) · COI 🥚 · **H** (content), L (one 2019 record's numbers unverified — 403) · bears_on: hyper-responders

### apoe4-2017 — genetic mechanism
- Calabuig-Navarro et al. 2017, *Sci Rep* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5343425/ · crossover + assays, n=31 men
- ApoE4 carriers: reduced LDL-receptor binding → lower LDL clearance → higher LDL-C. Mechanism for the hyper-responder subgroup. (No absolute LDL numbers; fat, not egg, challenge.)
- funding academic + Unilever · COI — · **H** (mechanism), M · bears_on: hyper-responders

## E · The crux — confounding, substitution logic & critiques

### zhong-jtd-response-2019 — the rebuttal (non-lipid mechanism)
- Zhong et al. 2019, *J Thorac Dis* — https://jtd.amegroups.org/article/view/31764/html · author response
- Argues egg–CVD association "remained after adjusting for baseline **serum** cholesterol" (≠ dietary chol adjustment), proposes **choline→TMAO**; 2 eggs/day vs none HR 1.27, "a reasonable effect size." Concedes observational limits.
- funding NIH · COI — · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, crux, mechanism(TMAO)

### kolb-dimarco-2023 — the confounding case `🥚`
- Kolb/DiMarco/Fernandez et al. 2023, *Curr Atheroscler Rep* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10285014/ · narrative review
- US cholesterol & saturated fat rise together (eggs eaten with bacon/sausage); of 13 US studies 7 harm / 6 null, of 8 Chinese 1 harm / 6 protective; only ~7% of studies adjusted for saturated fat. **The confounding argument is real but the review is egg-industry-funded.**
- funding **Egg Nutrition Center** · COI 🥚 · **H** · bears_on: confounding, COI

### ioannidis-2018 — noise > signal
- Ioannidis 2018, *JAMA* — https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2698337 · methodological editorial
- "The noise is much stronger than the signal, even under the best circumstances"; isolating one food from thousands co-consumed is "challenging, if not impossible." Undercuts confident causal claims on BOTH sides.
- funding academic (METRICS) · COI — · **H** · bears_on: crux, evidence-quality

### soliman-2018 — "no evidence" review
- Soliman 2018, *Nutrients* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6024687/ · narrative review
- "Current literature does not support the notion that dietary cholesterol increases CVD risk in healthy individuals"; homeostatic downregulation; LDL/HDL ratio ~constant. (Selective; concedes confounding & diabetes uncertainty.)
- funding institutional · COI — · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD

### harvard-nutritionsource-2019 — balanced commentary
- Harvard Chan / Willett-Hu 2019 — https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/2019/03/18/eggs-and-cholesterol-back-in-the-spotlight-in-new-jama-study/
- Reports Zhong's numbers; key caveat: "a single measure of diet... 30 years later"; Hu: eggs "can be included as part of a healthy eating pattern, but they are not essential." (Institutional stake — authored the competing BMJ null.)
- funding academic · COI — (institutional stake) · **H** · bears_on: F1-CVD, confounding

## F · Funding & outcome bias (symmetric COI ledger)

### lesser-2007 — foundational funding→conclusion bias
- Lesser et al. 2007, *PLoS Med* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1764435/ · 206 beverage articles
- Industry-funded interventional studies: 0% unfavorable conclusions vs 37% for independent (P=0.009); **OR of favorable conclusion 7.61** (1.27–45.73).
- funding foundation · COI — · **H** · bears_on: COI

### barnard-2021 — egg-specific `🌱`
- Barnard et al. 2021, *Am J Lifestyle Med* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7958219/ · 211 egg/cholesterol studies
- **49% of industry-funded studies drew conclusions discordant with their own results vs 13%** independent; industry funding rose to 60% by 2010–2019; >85% of ALL studies found unfavorable cholesterol effects. **Authors are PCRM (plant-based advocacy) — reverse COI.**
- funding none (PCRM) · COI 🌱 · **H** · bears_on: COI

### mandrioli-2016 — bias extends to reviews
- Mandrioli et al. 2016, *PLoS One* — https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0162198 · reviews of sweeteners
- Reviews by COI authors reached favorable conclusions 18/22 vs 4/9; **RR 7.36**; bias not explained by other risk-of-bias sources. (Sweeteners, transfers by analogy.)
- funding academic · COI — · **H** · bears_on: COI

## G · Non-CVD framings (F2–F5)

### vander-wal-2005 — satiety (eggs > bagel)
- Vander Wal et al. 2005, *JACN* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16373948/ · crossover, n=30 women
- Egg vs bagel breakfast → next-meal intake −685.7 kJ (P<0.0001), lower for 36 h.
- funding non-US-gov (unstated) · COI — (M) · **H** · bears_on: weight-satiety

### vander-wal-2008 — weight loss (only under restriction)
- Vander Wal et al. 2008, *Int J Obes* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18679412/ · RCT, n=152 · 8 wk
- Egg vs bagel **within a 1000-kcal-deficit diet**: weight −2.63 vs −1.59 kg. **NO effect without energy restriction.**
- funding NIH · COI — · **H** · bears_on: weight-satiety

### satiety-null-2022 — the counter-evidence
- 2022 crossover — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36237122/ · n=30 women
- Egg vs equal-protein cereal → no consistent satiety/intake advantage (Exp 1 null; Exp 2 fullness up but intake unchanged).
- funding not captured · **M** · bears_on: weight-satiety

### van-vliet-2017 — muscle (whole egg > white, but confounded)
- van Vliet et al. 2017, *AJCN* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28978542/ · crossover, n=10 trained men
- Whole egg > egg white for post-exercise myofibrillar protein synthesis (P=0.04, ~40% greater) — **but whole egg also delivered 17 g extra fat/energy (not matched); acute; n=10.**
- funding USDA · COI — (M; Egg Board promotes it) · **H** · bears_on: muscle-protein

### diaas-2024 — protein quality `🥚`
- Fanelli/Stein et al. 2024, *J Nutr Sci* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11658930/ · pig ileal model
- Egg DIAAS ~135% ("excellent"); PDCAAS 1.00. **But leucine ~0.55 g/egg is modest** — one egg doesn't hit the MPS leucine threshold.
- funding **Egg Nutrition Center** · COI 🥚 · **H** · bears_on: muscle-protein

### choline-2017 — real micronutrient case
- Wallace & Fulgoni 2017, *Nutrients* (NHANES) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5579632/
- ~125–147 mg choline/large egg; **only ~8% of US adults meet the AI**; egg consumers 525 vs 294 mg/day; "eggs the major US choline source." Important in pregnancy.
- funding authors have egg-industry consulting ties · COI 🥚 · **H** · bears_on: micronutrients

### lutein-2014 — eye (serum up, MPOD null at 90d)
- Kelly et al. 2014, *PLoS One* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3968018/ · RCT, n=97 · 90 d
- Enriched eggs raised serum lutein/zeaxanthin (+76%/+430%) but **no MPOD change**; normal egg raised serum lutein only NS.
- funding Newtricious + author patents · COI 🥚(commercial) · **H** · bears_on: micronutrients

### tmao-wilcox-2021 — the scare mostly doesn't fire
- Wilcox et al. (Hazen lab) 2021, *J Nutr* — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8410632/ · RCT, n=82 · 28 d
- **4 eggs/day did NOT raise fasting TMAO** (P=0.20); supplemental choline bitartrate did (1.9→11.1 µM). Whole eggs ≠ the TMAO problem in normal-renal adults.
- funding NIH · COI — · **H** · bears_on: micronutrients(TMAO)

### tang-2013 — the TMAO→CVD pathway (the other side)
- Tang et al. 2013, *NEJM* — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23614584/ · challenge + cohort n=4,007
- 2-egg phosphatidylcholine challenge → microbiota-dependent TMAO; **top-vs-bottom TMAO quartile MACE HR 2.54** (1.96–3.28). Establishes the hypothesis Wilcox 2021 partly defuses for whole eggs.
- funding NIH (Hazen holds TMAO patents) · COI — (patent) · **H** · bears_on: micronutrients(TMAO)

### salmonella-fda — food safety
- FDA Egg Safety Final Rule / CDC — https://www.fda.gov/food/egg-guidance-regulation-and-other-information/egg-safety-final-rule
- ~**1 in 20,000** eggs internally contaminated with Salmonella Enteritidis; cooking to ~71°C (firm yolk/white) or pasteurization eliminates risk. Highest-risk: young/elderly/pregnant/immunocompromised.
- funding US gov · COI — · **M** (pages blocked fetch; FDA-sourced figures) · bears_on: food-safety
